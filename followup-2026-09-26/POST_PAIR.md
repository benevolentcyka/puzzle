# Investigation after an assumed negative pair sweep

The requested working assumption is that the case-pair family returned no
solution. That assumption selects the next investigations; it is not recorded
as an observed exhaustive result. **The puzzle remains unsolved.**

## An arithmetic bug invalidated the earlier GPU negatives

The dependency dropped carries in its secp256k1 `reduce512` field reduction.
For entropy `bd6509467d92b47897f5b95558b79873`, the correct index-0 address is
`1MMxx4gAiieEdSTPcM5CNytn7grDNDwZvP`; the original GPU returned
`1FJmC9Xz8GQNcuAnBHQwiCUxNXzBqY3FKN`. Both implementations had the same
PBKDF2 seed. A step-by-step trace isolated the first wrong public key, before
the last normal BIP32 derivation. `diagnose-gpu.py` reproduces this witness.

The repaired reduction preserves both upper limbs and any subsequent folding
carry before conditional subtraction of the field prime. Its result passed
65,541 independent Python-integer modular-reduction comparisons, including
forced carry boundaries, and 8,192 full independent BIP44 key/public-key/address
comparisons. See `gpu-arithmetic-results.json` and `check-gpu-arithmetic.py`.
The root probe also passed 4,096 independent address comparisons.

The old process was stopped, and its checkpoint at 330,813,312/641,697,400 was
preserved in `../gpu-suspect-snapshots/four-groups-lflf-before-kernel-repair.json`.
**Those ranks cannot be counted as excluded candidates.** Other old snapshots
and the two initial pre-repair example results are retained with invalid
labels. CPU/Node searches are unaffected. All results below use corrected
kernel fingerprint `9429b7fc2cd5499ceb2993d2d375b8213ca7be61a6a2c4dae0ed1dc7cf0e0cd2`.

The pair runner now binds checkpoints to its kernels, tests 18 independent
CPU outputs at startup, and checks three actual MD5/address outputs in every
batch. A different or unrecorded kernel cannot silently inherit old progress.
The corrected pair sweep continues independently in this checkout; this
follow-up does not wait for it.

## Converter and source evidence

The eight wordlists are from the official
[July 2019 Ian Coleman commit](https://github.com/iancoleman/bip39/tree/45e40c288fe0d6cfba2c57a68f421eeb34d41385/src/js).
The seven non-English lists are Japanese, Spanish, simplified/traditional
Chinese, French, Italian and Korean. All 192 multilingual mnemonic and seed
fixtures in the official
[Trezor vectors](https://github.com/trezor/python-mnemonic/blob/master/vectors.json)
passed before the search. NFKD normalization and the SHA512 HMAC preprocessing
of passwords longer than 128 bytes are required and explicitly checked.

Fixed-word-count settings mean SHA256 of lowercase/uppercase **ASCII MD5
hex**, truncated to 16/20/24/28/32 bytes. The historical default was raw.
Manual language or length changes are hypotheses, not established author
settings. The encoding search's MD5 prefix is a restriction: it cannot
exclude a candidate outside that hint.

One wrong hint digit is justified as a bounded follow-up by the author's
[documented Block 59 correction](https://www.reddit.com/r/Grycoin/comments/c98uls/).
It is still an assumption about this example. The same correction is not
silently applied to a Bitcoin target address.

The example's archived unmodified bytes reproduce the published `7759227`
prefix, but the payout still does not reproduce in these finite families.
The strongest remaining evidence gap is the exact buffer hashed for the
example or final chapter, or the successful solver's actual method. This
does not prove the source must contain a large edit, or that the puzzle is
impossible. Failed direct Reddit retrieval (HTTP 403) is not proof that
no further hint exists. Available primary source and solver audits remain
in `research/SOLVER_SOURCE_AUDIT.md` and the root `AUTHOR_AUDIT.md`.

digit. That extra family has not been reported as complete in this pass.

resume their own fingerprinted checkpoint. A verified match is written only
to ignored `FOUND-*` files, including the exact witness bytes and mnemonic.

serialization and derivation settings.
